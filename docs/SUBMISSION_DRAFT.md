# Submission form

**Project Title:** GPU Value Scientist

**Description:**
GPU Value Scientist is an auditable AI research team for GPU rental prices. Four OpenAI agents propose forecasting ideas, find dated evidence through Exa inside the main loop, share hypotheses and verdicts through past.dev, and test ideas on later dates. Modal runs parallel experiments and hosts the public demo; development took place on Originator.

The selected submission contains 111 calibrated forecasts for 37 rental-price categories. In a historical follow-up, widening price ranges improved coverage from 64.70% to 75.42% and reduced pooled error by 4.323% against Pacific’s drift baseline. The uncertainty interval includes zero, some series worsen, and these are not November submission scores. The original autonomous loop did not establish a reliable improvement; calibration follow-ups were prompted by human feedback. A separately frozen group-specific alternative failed its promotion tests and was rejected.

Every source, decision, failed idea and comparison can be inspected. Pacific’s unchanged scorer supplies the metrics, forecasts reproduce offline, and the demo clearly distinguishes saved research results from new execution. past.dev matched local lookup accuracy (54/54), but was slower; no cost savings are established.

**Track:** Recursive Self-Improvement by Pacific — Direction A.

**Challenges:** Exa; Modal; past.dev (integration verified, accuracy/latency/cost advantage not established). Originator participation through this hackwithori.com session; no official eligibility or award claim.
