Three ideas the agents tested
1. Use recent prices: learn from the last 28 or 56 days instead of the full history.
2. Allow for prices that stay fixed: predict smaller changes when rental prices rarely move.
3. Adjust the price range: allow more room when prices fluctuate or earlier ranges missed the actual price.

By 2029, our central scenario puts H100 rentals at $0.98–$2.59 and A100 rentals at $0.42–$1.20 per GPU-hour, with lower earnings putting pressure on resale values.

The pack’s MLPerf benchmarks show H100 delivering about 2.46× A100’s inference throughput, illustrating why newer chips can undercut older chips’ rental earnings—and the resale value those earnings support.
