## 3. Filter rule and dropped rows

Every row originating from Vast.ai or RunPod is dropped from every shipped table, and every aggregate computed by this pack (the daily statistics and the recomputed cheapest series) is computed from the filtered rows, never filtered after the fact, because both providers' terms bar automated collection and compilation of their data (Vast.ai terms version 1 Sep 2026; RunPod terms 24 Mar 2026). The exception is stated below: four third-party aggregates ship as published, with their exposure flagged per row. Dropped-row counts per source and date are recorded in sources_register.csv (`rows_dropped`) and, per date and reason, in rental_observations_dropped_rows_by_source_date.csv.

Totals at the v0.1 freeze: afhubbard 113,161 rows (vastai 56,059; runpod 57,102); gpurentalprices 4,979 rows (RunPod; a further 660 AWS rows were excluded by the publisher's own errata as third-party readings, not by the filter); gpurentalprices_cheapest 816 RunPod-cheapest GPU-days (409 replaced by the cheapest non-RunPod offer recomputed from the filtered snapshots, 407 left as gaps); fastgpu_daily 7,876 rows (RunPod, all tiers, 5,648; Vast.ai 2,228); tanod 0 (neither provider appears); AWS spot ticks 0 (AWS-only source); hardware fundamentals, MLPerf capability rows 0 (neither provider appears). The standardized indices (Compute Screener, GIX, GPU Economy) and the GetDeploying weekly medians are third-party published aggregates: no shipped row originates from either venue, but each publisher's own panel includes one or both and a published value cannot be recomputed without them; every such row carries an `upstream_panel_includes` column so a stricter decision can be applied mechanically (source_ids computescreener, gix, gpueconomy_hf, gpueconomy_site_csv, getdeploying_weekly). The fastgpu fixings were left out of the pack on that same reasoning, so one owner decision is open and applies to all five: restore the fixings to index_prints.csv with the same column, or move the four aggregates to pointer rows (DATA.md section 5). Whether the exposure matters under Vast.ai's "any value derived therefrom" clause is the counsel read listed in DATA.md.
